Free Dispute Letter Templates

Four ready-to-use letters. Replace bracketed text, print or email, and send certified mail when possible. You can dispute for free yourself under the FCRA.

These templates are educational. They do not guarantee deletion of accurate information. Goodwill letters are discretionary and non-binding.

1. Credit Bureau Dispute Letter

FCRA § 611 
[Your Name]
[Your Address]
[City, State ZIP]
[Date]

[Bureau Name] — Dispute Department
[Bureau Address]

Re: Dispute of Inaccurate Information — File/Account #[Account Number]

Dear Sir or Madam:

I am writing to dispute the following information in my credit file. I have reviewed my report and believe the item below is inaccurate or incomplete:

- Creditor: [Creditor Name]
- Account #: [Account Number]
- Reason for dispute: [Explain why it is inaccurate — e.g., not mine, paid, duplicate, incorrect balance/date]

Under the Fair Credit Reporting Act, I request that you investigate this item and remove or correct it if it cannot be verified. Please provide written results of your investigation.

Sincerely,
[Your Name]
[SSN last 4: XXXX]
[DOB: MM/DD/YYYY]

2. Information Furnisher Dispute Letter

FCRA § 623 
[Your Name]
[Your Address]
[City, State ZIP]
[Date]

[Creditor/Furnisher Name] — Dispute Department
[Creditor Address]

Re: Dispute of Inaccurate Information Reported to Credit Bureaus — Account #[Account Number]

Dear Sir or Madam:

I dispute the accuracy of information you are furnishing about me. The item is inaccurate because: [reason].

Please investigate, correct your records, and notify the credit bureaus of the correction. Provide me written confirmation.

Sincerely,
[Your Name]
[Phone] [Email]

3. Goodwill Letter

Discretionary 
[Your Name]
[Your Address]
[City, State ZIP]
[Date]

[Creditor Name] — Goodwill Department
[Creditor Address]

Re: Request for Goodwill Adjustment — Account #[Account Number]

Dear [Creditor],

I have been a customer since [date] and have otherwise maintained a positive relationship. The late payment on [date] was an isolated incident caused by [brief reason]. I have since made all payments on time.

I respectfully request that you remove this late mark as a goodwill gesture. I understand this is at your discretion.

Thank you,
[Your Name]

4. Identity Theft / Fraudulent Account Dispute

FCRA § 605B 
[Your Name]
[Your Address]
[City, State ZIP]
[Date]

[Bureau Name] — Fraud Department
[Bureau Address]

Re: Identity Theft Report — Fraudulent Account #[Account Number]

Dear Sir or Madam:

I am a victim of identity theft. The following account does not belong to me:

- Creditor: [Creditor Name]
- Account #: [Account Number]
- Opened: [date] — I did not open this account.

I have filed a report with IdentityTheft.gov (Report #: [number]) and a police report (# [number] if available). Please block this information as required by the FCRA and send me written confirmation.

Sincerely,
[Your Name]
[ID attached: government ID, proof of address, police report]
