Free Credit Monitoring

Monitoring is reactive — it alerts you after activity. Pair it with a free credit freeze for the strongest protection. Below are the best free options and a built-in simulator.

Best Free Monitoring Stack (2026)

  • Credit Karma — Equifax + TransUnion, daily/weekly VantageScore, alerts.
  • Experian free — Real FICO Score 8, dark-web scan, Boost for rent/utilities.
  • Capital One CreditWise — TransUnion + Experian, no account required.
  • WalletHub — Rich free tier, daily updates, score simulator.
  • AnnualCreditReport.com — Free weekly full reports from all 3 bureaus.

No single free service covers all three bureaus with full features. Combine two or three.

Free Protection Checklist

Do these today — all free, all legal.

  1. Place a security freeze at Equifax, Experian, and TransUnion (online, permanent until lifted).
  2. Sign up for Credit Karma and the Experian free tier.
  3. Set a reminder to review full reports at AnnualCreditReport.com every few months.
  4. Enable two-factor authentication on email, banking, and card accounts.
  5. If theft occurs, start at IdentityTheft.gov.
