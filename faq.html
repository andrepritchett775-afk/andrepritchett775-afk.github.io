Frequently Asked Questions

Can you remove accurate negative information?

No. Only inaccurate, unverifiable, or outdated information can be removed. We never promise otherwise.

Can I dispute for free myself?

Yes. The FCRA gives you the right to dispute inaccurate items at no cost. Our service adds professional help.

How long does a dispute take?

Bureaus generally have 30 days (up to 45 with an extension) to investigate.

Do you guarantee a score increase?

No. Score changes depend on your full file and lender models. We focus on accuracy.

What is a credit freeze?

A free security freeze blocks most new creditors from accessing your report, preventing new fraudulent accounts.
